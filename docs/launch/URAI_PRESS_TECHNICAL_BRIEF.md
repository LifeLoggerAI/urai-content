# UrAi — Launch Press & Technical Brief

Status: launch-supporting narrative only. This document does not authorize deployment, billing, fundraising, legal-status claims, or release of non-final product imagery.

## What UrAi is

UrAi is a spatial personal-life runtime: passive and intentional signals become an explorable personal world rather than a conventional feed, dashboard, or chat history.

The core product language is spatial by design. Home leads into a navigable Life Map of galaxies, nebulae, memory stars, constellations, emotional weather, selected memories, Focus, and Replay. The product is intended to make a person's history something they can move through, revisit, understand, and carry forward.

UrAi is not presented as a single screen or a single model wrapper. The wider estate includes creator tooling, governed asset generation, professional/organizational surfaces, analytics, administration, accessibility systems, XR/WebXR architecture, and institutional/public surfaces.

## The launch story in one sentence

**UrAi turns passive life signals into a private, explorable personal universe — and the surrounding URAI ecosystem provides the creator, professional, governance, accessibility, and future immersive systems around it.**

## Why it is different

### Spatial memory, not a timeline

The canonical Life Map is a space/galaxy system: nebulae, memory stars, constellations, emotional weather, orbital travel, selected memory, Focus, and Replay. It is not a terrestrial map and should not be described as one.

### A system of systems

The consumer experience sits inside a broader architecture that includes UrAi Studio, governed asset/media workflows, professional/B2B surfaces, analytics, administration, support/communications, research/Labs surfaces, and an XR doorway.

### Privacy boundaries are architectural

Professional and organizational systems are designed around permissioned business metadata and aggregate context. Private memories, raw transcripts, biometrics, health details, and private life logs are not treated as generic enterprise data.

### Accessibility is structural

The product architecture includes reduced-motion and reduced-stimulation modes and is designed toward multimodal support for Deaf/hard-of-hearing, Blind/low-vision, neurodivergent, PTSD-sensitive, and TBI-sensitive use. Public claims must distinguish implemented capabilities from future concepts and must not make unsupported medical claims.

### Built toward immersive computing

The source estate includes a WebXR/Quest entry architecture and capability/fallback paths. Earlier retained evidence records a successful immersive session on physical Quest hardware. Final launch claims should still be bound to the exact deployed release being demonstrated rather than treating historical proof as current-release certification.

## Press FAQ

### Is UrAi just another AI chat app?

No. Its defining interaction model is a spatial personal world built around memory, context, movement, selection, Focus, and Replay. AI is part of the runtime and orchestration layer rather than the entire user interface.

### Is the Life Map a map of physical places?

No. The canonical Life Map is cosmic: galaxies, nebulae, memory stars, constellations, emotional weather, orbital travel, and selected memories. Real-world/location context is a separate concern.

### Is the B2B product using private user memories?

The professional architecture is designed around business metadata, permissioned/aggregate context, account readiness, governance and integration health. Private memories and raw life data are outside that enterprise boundary unless a future capability has separate explicit consent and authority.

### Does UrAi support VR today?

The estate includes WebXR/Quest entry architecture and retained evidence of an immersive Quest session. Any launch-day compatibility statement should be verified against the exact deployed release and device being shown.

### Is everything already deployed?

No blanket claim should be made. URAI deliberately separates source-green, visual acceptance, independent review, governance, protected deployment, and live readback. Each surface should be described according to its current evidence.

### Is FINITE TIME the product?

No. FINITE TIME is a separate cinematic/asset-production artifact that can support launch storytelling when its current production, literal-QC, privacy/likeness, and release authority permit.
